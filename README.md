# Delta for Android

Delta turns your fitness tracker's data into a daily **recovery** score, **sleep** and **strain**, plus Pulse Age, stress and live heart rate. It reads from **Health Connect**, so it works with most trackers that sync there (Fitbit / Google, Samsung, Oura, Polar and others).

Delta is in **early access**. Expect rough edges, and please tell us what breaks.

## Install

1. Open the **[latest release](../../releases/latest)** on your phone and tap the file ending in **.apk** to download it.
2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow your browser or files app to **install unknown apps**, then go back and tap **Install**.
3. If Play Protect warns that it doesn't recognise the app, tap **More details → Install anyway**. This happens because Delta isn't on the Play Store yet.
4. Open Delta and connect Health Connect. In your tracker's app, make sure syncing to Health Connect is turned on.

Needs Android 9 or newer.

## Updates

When a new version is out, Delta shows a card on the **Today** screen. Tap **Update**, then **Install**. Your data stays as it is.

The first time, Android asks you to allow Delta to install updates. That switch is only used for Delta's own updates.

## Privacy

Your health data is read from Health Connect and processed on your phone. The only thing Delta fetches from the internet is this page's latest release, to check for updates.

Scores are estimates for wellbeing, not medical advice.

## What we're working on

**Delta insights:** on-device machine learning that learns your personal patterns from your own nights, starting with how your bedtime affects your HRV and recovery. Everything is trained and runs on your phone; no data is sent anywhere.
