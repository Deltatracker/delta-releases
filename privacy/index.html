Delta privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Delta is a free app that shows your health and fitness data. It is built so that your data stays on your phone.

The short version

  • No account, no sign-up.
  • Your health data stays on your phone. Delta has no servers and sends your data nowhere.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking.

What Delta reads, and why

From Health Connect, only the types you allow: steps, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, sleep, exercise, oxygen saturation, respiratory rate, skin temperature, VO₂ max, floors, calories, weight, water and nutrition (food you or other apps have logged). Delta uses them to show your recovery, strain, sleep, food and trends. If you allow background access, Delta also refreshes them while the app is closed, so widgets and reminders stay current.

What you enter in Delta: daily check-ins, notes, journal moments (your words, photos, how it felt, tags and place), workouts you time in Delta, cycle days if you use Circa, your birth date, medicines and doses, water, food and fasting plans, goals and settings. These are stored on your phone only. Each journal moment also keeps your heart rate, stress and Charge at that minute, worked out on your phone.

Your camera, only when you take a photo for your journal. Photos are kept in Delta's private storage on your phone. You can also pick a photo from your gallery; Delta only sees the one you choose.

Your microphone, only while you hold the mic to speak an entry. Your words are understood on your phone by Android's on-device speech recognition, and the recording is not kept. If your phone can't do this offline, Delta tells you instead of sending your voice anywhere.

Your approximate location, only when you save a journal moment and only if you allow it. To turn it into a place name such as a neighbourhood, Delta passes the coordinates to Android's built-in geocoder, which may ask Google's location service. Nothing else is sent with them. Names you give places are remembered on your phone. You can turn places off in Delta or in Android settings at any time.

Music in your day, only if you turn it on. Delta uses notification access to see which song is playing, so it can show it in your day. It reads nothing else, and what you listened to stays on your phone.

Your calendar, only if you turn on calendar-aware reminders. Delta reads busy times on your phone to stay quiet during meetings and travel. Events never leave your phone.

Bluetooth, only for live heart rate. Delta connects to your tracker's heart-rate broadcast on your phone.

Δt (Earned time), only if you turn it on. Delta uses Android's usage access and Accessibility service to see which app is on your screen, and, only if you turn on Websites, the address in your browser's address bar. Delta uses this only to count your minutes on the apps and sites you chose and to stop them when your earned minutes run out. Delta doesn't read anything else on your screen, doesn't act for you, and keeps only per-app minute totals on your phone. None of it is sent anywhere. You can turn Δt off in You, and switch off the Accessibility service in Android's settings at any time.

Your phone's motion sensors, only if you allow physical activity access. Delta reads the step counter and air-pressure sensor to time move reminders and count floors. The readings stay on your phone.

Backups and sharing you start

You can export a backup file from Delta and import it again later, or have Delta keep weekly copies in a folder you choose. Backups include your journal entries, but not their photos. The file is saved where you choose, and Delta never uploads it anywhere.

When you export your daily scores or share a badge, Delta hands just that to the app you pick (for example email or a chat app). Nothing goes out unless you tap share.

What Delta writes

If you allow it, water and food you log in Delta are saved to Health Connect, so your other health apps can see them. Workouts you time in Delta stay in Delta. Nothing else is written.

Internet use

Delta only reads public files from GitHub:

  • An announcement file, for news about Delta (for example a beta).
  • Past release notes, when you open What's new.
  • In the version installed from GitHub only: a check for a newer version, and the download itself.

These requests contain nothing about you or your health. As with any website, GitHub can see your IP address when your phone fetches a file.

Sharing

Delta does not share, sell or transfer your data to anyone.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Delta, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes everything Delta stored. Data in Health Connect stays under your control in Health Connect's own settings, where you can also remove Delta's access at any time.

Not medical advice

Delta shows estimates to help you understand your habits. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat anything. Talk to a doctor about your health.

Children

Delta is meant for adults and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date, and big changes will be mentioned in the app's What's new.
