Delta privacy policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Delta is a free app that shows your health and fitness data. It is built so that your data stays on your phone.

The short version

  • No account, no sign-up.
  • Your health data stays on your phone. Delta has no servers and sends your data nowhere.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking.

What Delta reads, and why

From Health Connect, only the types you allow: steps, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, sleep, exercise, oxygen saturation, respiratory rate, skin temperature, VO₂ max, floors, calories, water and nutrition (food you or other apps have logged). Delta uses them to show your recovery, strain, sleep, food and trends. If you allow background access, Delta also refreshes them while the app is closed, so widgets and reminders stay current.

What you enter in Delta: daily check-ins, notes, medicines and doses, water, food and fasting plans, goals and settings. These are stored on your phone only.

Your calendar, only if you turn on calendar-aware reminders. Delta reads busy times on your phone to stay quiet during meetings and travel. Events never leave your phone.

Bluetooth, only for live heart rate. Delta connects to your tracker's heart-rate broadcast on your phone.

Your phone's motion sensors, only if you allow physical activity access. Delta reads the step counter and air-pressure sensor to time move reminders and count floors. The readings stay on your phone.

Backups and sharing you start

You can export a backup file from Delta and import it again later. The file is saved where you choose, and Delta never uploads it anywhere.

When you export your daily scores or share a badge, Delta hands just that to the app you pick (for example email or a chat app). Nothing goes out unless you tap share.

What Delta writes

If you allow it, water and food you log in Delta are saved to Health Connect, so your other health apps can see them. Nothing else is written.

Internet use

Delta only reads public files from GitHub:

  • An announcement file, for news about Delta (for example a beta).
  • Past release notes, when you open What's new.
  • In the version installed from GitHub only: a check for a newer version, and the download itself.

These requests contain nothing about you or your health. As with any website, GitHub can see your IP address when your phone fetches a file.

Sharing

Delta does not share, sell or transfer your data to anyone.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Delta, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes everything Delta stored. Data in Health Connect stays under your control in Health Connect's own settings, where you can also remove Delta's access at any time.

Not medical advice

Delta shows estimates to help you understand your habits. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat anything. Talk to a doctor about your health.

Children

Delta is meant for adults and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date, and big changes will be mentioned in the app's What's new.
